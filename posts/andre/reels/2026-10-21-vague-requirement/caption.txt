Client: “Make it user-friendly.” 😅
BA: “What does that mean?”
Client: “Fast. Fast-fast.”
BA: “How fast is fast-fast?”
Client: “Yesterday.”

Every Business Analyst has been here. The fix? Turn vague wishes into testable sentences with Given / When / Then. ✅
